About Trail Maintenance Advocacy

Let's care together

Love to hike? Garibaldi Provincial Park offers over 90km of hiking trails in beautiful terrain. Those trails need to be maintained.

We are a group of volunteers who support Garibaldi Park through trail building and maintenance work. Our advocacy committee also works on behalf of park users to improve access and ensure the trails and facilities stay in good shape so that everybody can benefit from them.

What We Do

The Friends of Garibaldi Park Society's Advocacy Committee champions the interests of park users by identifying and addressing issues that affect access to and use of Garibaldi Provincial Park. We work collaboratively with BC Parks, local communities, and stakeholders to raise awareness of critical topics, define positive outcomes, and take action to achieve meaningful change.

Our advocacy efforts ensure that the voices of park users are heard in decision-making processes, helping to shape policies and initiatives that balance conservation with sustainable public access to this spectacular wilderness.
